# Reporting notes

The date applies to what happened or was announced on Tuesday, 24 November 2026. The illustrations are symbolic; none depict an identified person, vehicle, or building.

- **Ukraine.** On 24 November, officials put the toll from the Russian strike on a Zaporizhzhia apartment building at two killed and 48 wounded. The strike itself was late on 23 November, local time; the 24 November development is the updated toll.[17]
- **U.S. / China.** A federal jury returned guilty verdicts against Huawei and three subsidiaries, including on charges involving U.S. sanctions on Iran and bank fraud.[16]
- **Yemen.** The UK announced sanctions on the Houthis and four officials after renewed attacks in Yemen and on Saudi Arabia. It also cited attacks on commercial shipping in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and serious human rights abuses.[3]
- **China / Pakistan.** China announced the Shenzhou-24 crew. Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali was named as a payload specialist and was set to become the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong. The story is about the crew announcement, not a launch on the 24th.[6]
- **DR Congo.** The WHO director-general announced the safe evacuation of WHO staff, Ministry of Health staff and colleagues from Akwe hospital in Ituri amid renewed fighting. The ambulance is a symbol, not a claim about how staff were transported.[7]
- **Senegal / Morocco.** The Court of Arbitration for Sport overturned an appeal ruling and restored Senegal as the winner of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. The ruling, not the match, took place on 24 November 2026.[8]
- **Colombia.** On the tenth anniversary of the government's peace agreement with the former FARC-EP, the UN secretary-general called for renewed commitment to implementing the agreement in full.[12]
